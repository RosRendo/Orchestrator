# RendoOS: The Founder Operating System — Master Architecture

## Core Philosophy
> **"Hermes is not your brain. GPT is not your brain. Claude is not your brain. DeepSeek is not your brain. They are workers. Your Obsidian knowledge, experiences, decisions, skills, and business data are the persistent layer."**

RendoOS is not merely a coding assistant or a developer dashboard—it is a **Personal & Business Operating System for Founders**.

## 2. The 6-Zone Visual & Functional Hierarchy

### Zone 1: Overview & Work — *"What am I doing right now?"*
- **🏠 Home**: Founder Pulse, Today's Top Mission, Business Highlights, Real-time telemetry.
- **📁 Workspaces**: Active software projects & repositories.
- **💻 Console & Terminals**: Fullscreen console and floating terminals.

### Zone 2: AI & Skill HQ ⭐ — *"How does RendoOS help me do it?"*
- **🧩 Skill Hub**: 14 functional departments, dynamic skill levels (1–10), playbooks, and exercises.
- **🤖 Agent Control**: Running task queue and active agent workers.
- **🎯 Task Missions**: Multi-agent missions and autonomous workflows.
- **🧠 AI Workspace**: Prompt workbench and multi-model routing lab.
- **💻 Coding Agents**: Hermes, OpenCode, Aider, Cline.

### Zone 3: Developer & Infrastructure — *"How is my technical infrastructure running?"*
- **🐳 Docker Manager**: Container states and orchestration.
- **🚀 Deployment Cockpit**: Production pipelines and machines.
- **🔐 Env Manager**: Keyrings and configuration secrets.
- **🗄️ Database Explorer**: Prisma Studio data explorer.
- **📡 API Client**: Request workbench.
- **🐙 Git & GitHub**: Workspace version control.
- **⚡ n8n Workflows**: Automation triggers and webhooks.
- **📈 Trading Agent**: MT5 trading bridge.

### Zone 4: Knowledge & Memory — *"What does RendoOS know?"*
- **📓 Obsidian Vault**: Single source of truth (`D:\AI\Obsidian`).
- **📑 Notion Workspace**: External docs and project management.
- **🧠 AI Intelligence Layer**: Semantic index and memory layers.
- **📚 Research Repository**: Competitor audits, market analyses.
- **📝 Daily Journal**: Morning focus and evening reflections (`/daily`, `/daily_review`).
- **🎯 Goals & Decisions**: High-stakes architectural and business decision log.

### Zone 5: Business HQ 💼 — *"How am I building and monetizing ventures?"*
- **💡 Idea Pipeline**: 10-stage progression from raw thought to automated cash flow.
- **🎯 Leads & Customers**: Founder CRM, discovery call notes, objections.
- **💰 Sales & Playbooks**: Cold outreach scripts, pitch decks, closing frameworks.
- **📣 Marketing & Growth**: Content distribution, viral loops, paid channels.
- **💵 Finance & MRR**: Revenue tracking, runway, unit economics.

### Zone 6: External AI — *"What external tool portals can I access?"*
- ChatGPT, Claude Web, Perplexity, Cursor, v0, etc.

## 3. The 10-Stage "Idea → Business" Pipeline

Every software product and SaaS project follows this immutable validation pipeline:

```
💡 IDEA ➔ 🔬 RESEARCH ➔ 🗣️ INTERVIEW ➔ 🎯 VALIDATED ➔ 🛠️ MVP
   ➔ 💳 FIRST PAYMENT ➔ 👥 10 CUSTOMERS ➔ 📈 GROWTH ➔ ⚙️ AUTOMATE
```

This prevents the classic builder trap: *27 codebases, 0 paying customers.*
