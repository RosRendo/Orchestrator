# Avoid Trades During High-Impact USD News Releases



### [2026-09-07] Avoid Trades During High-Impact USD News Releases
- **Core Principle**: High-impact news events cause extreme volatility, unpredictable spreads, and false breakouts, making entries during these periods statistically unfavorable.
- **Actionable Rule**: Before any trade, check the economic calendar for red-folder USD events (CPI, NFP, FOMC). If one is imminent (within 30 minutes) or occurring, stand aside. After the release, wait at least 15 minutes for spreads to normalize and for liquidity sweeps to form a clear direction before considering an entry.
- **Context / Evidence**: Observed repeated losses and slippage when trading during CPI/NFP/FOMC releases; subsequent discipline of waiting 15 minutes improved fill quality and trade outcomes.
