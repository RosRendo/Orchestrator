# Never Discount Core Offering Without Concessions



### [2026-09-07] Never Discount Core Offering Without Concessions
- **Core Principle**: Discounting the core product without concessions devalues your offering and sets a precedent for future negotiations, eroding profit margins and perceived value.
- **Actionable Rule**: When a prospect asks for a discount, do not reduce price in isolation. Instead, respond with: 'We can adjust pricing if we remove X feature' or 'We can offer a discount if you commit to an annual upfront payment.' Always tie any price reduction to a specific concession that protects value.
- **Context / Evidence**: Real-world sales experience showing that discounts without concessions lead to margin erosion and a culture of discount-seeking, while conditional discounts maintain value and improve cash flow.
